CHAPTER III: RENT

Section 11: Security Deposit

The security deposit to be paid by the tenant in advance shall be such as may be agreed upon between the landlord and the tenant in the tenancy agreement.

For residential premises, the security deposit shall not exceed two months' rent.

For non-residential premises, the security deposit shall not exceed six months' rent.

The security deposit shall be refunded to the tenant on the date of taking over vacant possession of the premises by the landlord, after making due deduction of any liability of the tenant.

IMPORTANT APPLICABILITY NOTE:
This is the Model Tenancy Act, 2021 issued by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs as a model law. Its provisions should not be treated as automatically applicable across every State or Union Territory. Actual applicability depends on the tenancy law adopted or notified in the relevant State or Union Territory.
